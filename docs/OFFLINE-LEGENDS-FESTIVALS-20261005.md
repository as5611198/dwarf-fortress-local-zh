# Complete festival activities and native entity/world introductions

The shared Native/Broker composer supports complete poetry recitals,
performances, story recitals, ceremonies and processions returning to their
starting building, including finite complete feature lists. It also supports
civilization/group introductions with a reviewed vanilla race and verified
world identity. Unknown programs, missing names, unrecognized routes, malformed
lists and extra aftermath preserve the entire native paragraph.

The Legends adapter reads the current entity's native occasions, referenced
poetic/musical/dance forms and site buildings once per page capture. Repeated
references share bounded local lookups. Only exact name spans become local DFT
slots; unmatched prose remains explicit. Original source, native word records,
link IDs/types and unlinked palette boundaries stay intact. DFT names never gain
click targets or enter the remote rich-text protocol. No per-frame world scans
or I/O were added.
